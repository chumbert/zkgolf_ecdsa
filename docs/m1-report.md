## Axiom audit

All M1 public roots depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
`native_decide` is **not** used anywhere in `StandardECDSA/`: it introduces a
fresh axiom (`..._native.native_decide.ax_...`), which the release audit forbids.
All concrete computations use kernel `decide`.

## Blocking item: the 1,039-slot enumeration (T13)

The plan's M1 gate asks for the 1,039-exception coverage to be complete **or
explicitly reported as blocking with the exact obstruction**.  It is blocking:

> The 1,039 slots are indexed by the *actual affine operations* of the
> implementation: the initial fixed-base addition, the 128 window additions, the
> six shifted-table additions and the 256 ELM steps.  Those operations live in
> `Phases/FixedBase.lean`, `Phases/ShiftedTables.lean` and
> `Phases/JointLadder.lean`, which are M2/M3 deliverables and do not exist yet.
> A coverage theorem "every failure of admissibility implies one listed
> equation" cannot be stated, let alone proved, before the operations it
> quantifies over are defined.

What *is* in place: the generic counting theorem
(`exists_avoiding_injective_equations`), the injectivity of `k • ·` for
`0 < k < n` (`nsmul_injective_of_lt`), the `BadFamily`/`GoodOffset` interface,
and the same-`x`-implies-equal-or-negative bridge.  Instantiating the family with
the 1,039 slots and the 1,040 candidates is T13/T14.
